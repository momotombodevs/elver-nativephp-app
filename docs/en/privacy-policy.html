Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Contact: privacy@momotombo.dev

Elver is a weather app for iOS and Android. It lets you check forecasts for saved places, keep the latest available data offline, and create local weather alerts.

Data Elver processes

Location. When you choose “Use current location,” Elver requests foreground location permission to obtain a coordinate and save it as a place. It does not request or use background location to track your movements. You may also add a place manually.

Weather data. Elver requests data from Open-Meteo for the latitude and longitude of the selected place, including temperature, humidity, precipitation, wind, apparent temperature, and UV index. The latest valid result is stored locally so it can be viewed offline.

Alerts and notifications. Alerts are configured on the device using weather thresholds. Notifications are local; they are not push messages sent from an Elver server. The operating system may delay checks because of battery restrictions, permissions, or lack of connectivity.

Elver does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, audio, or advertising identifiers. The current version does not include advertising or tracking profiles.

Storage and providers

Places, forecasts, preferences, and alerts are stored in SQLite on your device. Uninstalling the app may remove these data according to the operating system's behavior.

To retrieve a forecast, Elver sends Open-Meteo the coordinates of the selected place and the required query parameters. Please also review Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.

Purpose and your choices

  • Show the forecast for the place you selected.
  • Keep the latest available forecast for offline use.
  • Evaluate the alerts you configured and show local notifications.

You can refuse or revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings. You can also edit or delete places and alerts, or clear all local storage by uninstalling Elver.

Security, children, and changes

App data stays in the device's protected storage. We cannot guarantee the security of information you send by email outside the device. Elver is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly request their data.

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal obligations change. We will publish the current version at this URL and update the revision date.

Elver does not replace official emergency alerts or public safety weather services.
